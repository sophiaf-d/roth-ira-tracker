Strategy & data

How the alerts work

  • BUY when the price is above its trend MA, RSI is under the cap, and either the fast MA crosses above the slow MA or the price closes at a new breakout high.
  • SELL when the price drops by the trailing-stop % from its high since the BUY, closes below the trend MA, or the fast MA crosses back under the slow MA.
  • Long-only, no margin, no shorting, just like a Roth IRA cash account.
